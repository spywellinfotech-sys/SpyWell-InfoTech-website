Digital service

Our plans are digital services that start as soon as the payment is confirmed. Because of this, payments for plans that have been activated are generally non-refundable.

When we do refund

  • Your money was deducted but the plan did not start.
  • You were charged twice for the same plan.
  • You were charged a wrong amount because of an error on our side.

For these cases, contact us within 7 days of the payment with your registered email or mobile number, the date and amount, and the payment or UPI reference number. After we verify it, the refund (or the correct plan activation) is done within 7 working days. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

Cancellation

You can stop using a plan at any time. Cancelling does not give a refund for the remaining period, and the plan stays active until its end date. Plans do not auto-renew; you choose when to renew.

Wallet credit

Referral wallet credit has no cash value and is not refundable or withdrawable. If a payment that used wallet credit is refunded, the credit is returned to the wallet.

Free periods

Offers and free periods have no cash value and can be changed or ended at any time.
