Frequently asked questions

What is a mini website?

It is a simple one-page website that carries your business details, offers and contact buttons, built to be shared by link or QR.

Do I need technical skills?

No. Everything is filled in from forms on your phone.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes, your own domain is supported on the website plan.

Is it good for Google search?

Each site is a normal public page with your business name, so people searching your name can find it.
