What you get

  • Save any LiveCard you scan or open

How to get started

  1. Scan or open a LiveCard and save it

Frequently asked questions

Can I import from my phone contacts?

Yes, on supported Android phones you can pick a contact from your phone list while adding a new one.

Do saved cards update by themselves?

Yes. Saved LiveCards show the latest details the owner publishes.

Is my network private?

Your saved list is visible only to you.

Can I set reminders?

Yes, use FollowUp to schedule calls and tasks.
