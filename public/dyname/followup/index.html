FollowUp: never miss a customer call back

FollowUp is a light CRM inside LiveCard. After each call you record how it went and set the next follow-up, so no enquiry goes cold.

हर ग्राहक के लिए कॉल का नतीजा लिखिए, अगली कॉल की तारीख़ तय कीजिए और रिमाइंडर पाइए। छोटा, आसान CRM।
Start free

What you get

  • Call outcome after every call: interested, call later, busy and more
  • Schedule the next follow-up date and time
  • Open task list of everything due
  • Call and WhatsApp from the same screen
  • Works with your saved network and leads

How to get started

  1. Pick a contact from My Network or Leads
  2. Call and record the outcome
  3. Set the next follow-up and get reminded

Frequently asked questions

Is FollowUp free for every user?

It is available to every logged-in LiveCard user.

Can I track outcomes?

Yes, choose an outcome after each call and add a note.

Does it replace a full CRM?

It is built for small businesses that need simple call tracking, not enterprise CRM.

Where do my contacts come from?

From your saved network, leads and manually added contacts.
