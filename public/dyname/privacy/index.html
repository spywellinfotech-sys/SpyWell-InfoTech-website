1. Who we are

This policy covers the DynaMe app, the SpyWell Digital Studio website (spywell.in/studio), digital visiting cards, mini websites, invitation cards and related pages, operated by SpyWell InfoTech, Saharanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile identifier. You may also add your mobile number.
  • Card and website content: name, business name, designation, phone, WhatsApp, email, address, website, social links, photos, logo, services, event details and any text you add to your cards, mini websites or invitations.
  • Card scans: if you use the "scan a card" feature, the photo of the card you upload is sent to an AI service to read the text, and the extracted details are filled in for you. We use the image only to read it for you.
  • Visitor and enquiry details: if a card owner turns on the visitor gate or the enquiry button, the name, mobile number and message that a visitor types are stored and shown to that card owner.
  • Usage data: counts of card views and button clicks (call, WhatsApp, map, save contact, share), and basic technical data such as device and browser type, to show card owners their insights and to keep the service running.
  • Payments: payments are processed by Razorpay (or by UPI that you pay directly). We receive the payment status, amount and payment reference. We do not receive or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login details.
  • Referral and wallet: referral codes, who referred whom, and wallet credits.
  • Saved items on your device: we use your browser storage (for example to remember your language, login session and a referral code) so the app works properly. We do not use it for advertising.

3. How we use information

  • To create, show and share your digital cards, mini websites and invitations.
  • To sign you in and keep your account safe.
  • To process payments, subscriptions and referral rewards.
  • To show you insights about your own cards.
  • To provide support, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account or service changes.

4. What others can see

Anything you publish on a card, mini website, invitation or in the business directory can be opened and seen by anyone who has the link, and a listing in the directory can be found by the public. Please do not publish information you do not want shared. You can unpublish a card or remove a directory listing at any time.

5. Sharing with service providers

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with providers that help us run the service: Google (sign-in), Supabase (database, login and file storage), Razorpay (payments), GitHub Pages and similar hosting, and an AI service for the card-scan feature. They process data only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information if the law or a government authority requires it.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can edit or delete your cards yourself in the app. To delete your account data, email us from your registered email address or message us on WhatsApp, and we will remove your cards, profile and wallet data. Some records, such as payment records, may be kept as long as the law requires.

7. Security

We use access controls and secure connections (HTTPS) to protect your data. No online service can be completely secure, so please keep your Google account safe and share your card links carefully.

8. Children

The service is meant for adults and businesses. It is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Your choices

  • View and change your profile and cards in the app.
  • Ask us for a copy or removal of your data.
  • Withdraw consent by stopping use of the service and asking us to delete your data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to use the service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
