Frequently asked questions

Is the digital visiting card free?

Yes. You can create a live card with your own link for free and try every plan for 7 days.

Do people need to install an app to see my card?

No. The card opens in any phone browser from a link, QR code or NFC tap.

Can I change my details after publishing?

Yes. Edit the card any time and the same link and QR show the updated details immediately.

Can I print the QR code?

Yes. Open My card, tap the QR button, then download or print it.
