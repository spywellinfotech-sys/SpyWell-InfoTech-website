How LiveCard works

LiveCard gives you one identity that works across a card, website, invitation and more. Here is the whole flow from sign-up to sharing and tracking.

What you get

  • Sign up with your mobile number
  • Pick a template and add your details
  • Publish to get your own link and QR
  • Share on WhatsApp, print the QR or write an NFC tag
  • See views, QR scans and NFC taps in insights
  • Save and manage contacts in My Network and FollowUp

How to get started

  1. Sign up and choose what to make
  2. Fill details and publish
  3. Share and track

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to make a card?

Usually a few minutes.

What can I make with LiveCard?

Visiting cards, mini websites, invitations, greeting cards, address cards and car call QR.

Do I need to install an app?

You can use the web app or the Android app. People viewing your card need nothing.
