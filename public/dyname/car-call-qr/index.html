Car Call QR: let people reach you when your vehicle blocks the way

Print a QR sticker for your car, bike or auto. Anyone who needs to reach the owner scans it and gets a simple call page, so you do not have to leave your number on the dashboard.

What you get

  • One QR per vehicle with your vehicle number
  • Scanner gets a clear call button
  • Download and print the QR as a sticker
  • Works with any phone camera
  • Update your contact number without changing the sticker

How to get started

  1. Open Car Call QR in the app
  2. Enter vehicle number and contact
  3. Download, print and stick the QR on your vehicle

Frequently asked questions

Does the person scanning need an app?

No. Any phone camera opens the call page.

Can I change my phone number later?

Yes. The QR stays the same and the page shows your updated contact.

Is it only for cars?

No. Use it for bikes, autos, trucks and any vehicle.

Can I print it myself?

Yes. Download the QR and print it on a sticker or lamination.
