Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers.

What is DynaMe?

DynaMe is an app by SpyWell InfoTech to create and share a digital visiting card, mini website, invitation cards, home address card and car call QR, and to save other people's business cards.

Is DynaMe free to use?

You can open the app and start with a digital card at spywell.in/studio. Some features, such as the Mini Website enquiry inbox or your own domain, depend on the plan. Plan details are shown inside the app.

How can I create a digital visiting card?

Open the app, create your profile, fill in your details and publish. Your card gets a shareable link and QR code.

Can I create my own mini website?

Yes. Your card details are turned into a mini website with its own link. The enquiry form is available on eligible Mini Website plans.

Can I share my card on WhatsApp?

Yes. Share through WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, SMS, email and other apps, or copy the link.

Can I create wedding invitation cards?

Yes. You can create digital invitation cards for weddings, engagements, birthdays, anniversaries, family functions and other celebrations.

Can I save someone else's business card?

Yes. Open a DynaMe card and tap Save, add a contact by hand, or scan a physical card.

What is the AI Card Scanner?

You photograph a physical visiting card and AI fills in the details it can read. You review and save. Currently up to 30 scans per day.

Is my card automatically visible in the Business Directory?

No. A card is listed only after you give consent.

Does DynaMe support Android?

Yes, DynaMe has an Android app (package com.spywell.vicard). The web version also works in your browser at spywell.in/studio.
