Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers.

What is LiveCard?

LiveCard is an app to create and share a digital visiting card, mini website, invitation cards, home address card and car call QR, and to save other people's business cards.

Is LiveCard free to use?

You can open the app and start with a digital card at livecard.in/studio. Some features, such as the Mini Website enquiry inbox or your own domain, depend on the plan. Plan details are shown inside the app.

How can I create a digital visiting card?

Open the app, create your profile, fill in your details and publish. Your card gets a shareable link and QR code.

Can I create my own mini website?

Yes. Your card details are turned into a mini website with its own link. The enquiry form is available on eligible Mini Website plans.

Can I share my card on WhatsApp?

Yes. Share through WhatsApp, SMS and other apps, as a card photo or QR code, or copy the link. You can also enter a mobile number to open WhatsApp with your link ready.

Can I create wedding invitation cards?

Yes. You can create digital invitation cards for weddings, engagements, birthdays, anniversaries, family functions and other celebrations.

Can I save someone else's business card?

Yes. Open a LiveCard card and tap Save, add a contact by hand, or scan a physical card.

How does card scanning work?

You photograph a physical visiting card and save it with the contact. The photo is stored privately and you can open it from the saved contact. No AI is used and there is no charge.

Is my card automatically visible in the Business Directory?

No. A card is listed only after you give consent.

Does LiveCard support Android?

Yes, LiveCard has an Android app. The web version also works in your browser at livecard.in/studio.
