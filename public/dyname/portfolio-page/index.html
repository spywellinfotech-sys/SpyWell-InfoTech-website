Frequently asked questions

Who is a Portfolio Page for?

It is for people who do a job or offer a skill, such as employees, designers, developers, teachers and freelancers.

How is it different from a Mini Website?

A Mini Website is for a business or shop with products and services. A Portfolio Page is a personal profile for a working professional.

Is it free to start?

Yes, you can start free from the Studio.

Can I share it as a QR code?

Yes, every card gets its own link and QR code.
